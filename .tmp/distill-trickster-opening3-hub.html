[Grinning Trickster Stirs Up Trouble] Opening: Top track hybrid (Major Powers)

Oct 15, 2023

Unique Powers reminder

    

Opening

Growth

Turn 1

G3; G2 top.

Play:

  • If drawn another : Impersonate authority and Overenthusiastic arson to unlock Let’s see what happens level 1.
  • Otherwise: substitute either with Unexpected tigers.

Turn 2

G2 bottom; G3.

Play Incite the mob and another to unlock Let’s see what happens level 2.

Turn 3

Reclaim; G2 bottom.

Play 2. Make sure you have 212 to unlock Let’s see what happens level 2.

Turn 4

G2 bottom; G3 Major, forget from discard if possible to have 5 cards in hand.

Play 3, preferably unlocking Let’s see what happens level 2.

Turn 5

G2 top; G3 Minor.

Play 3.

Remarks

On turn 5 there are a few options:

  • G2+G4 if Energy is needed, but in that case only 2 cards can be played
  • G1+G2 if needing to reclaim.
