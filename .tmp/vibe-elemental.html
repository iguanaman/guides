The systems that decide games — fear economy, blight cascades, and ravage/build timing.

Fear economy & terror levels▸
Fear is a parallel win condition and a tempo lever. Generating it early buys terror-level cards that thin the invader deck and can end the game before blight overwhelms you. Learn which spirits bank fear vs. spend it, and when a fear victory is actually faster than a full clear.
