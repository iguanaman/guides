Welcome!

Hello there, and welcome! This is a hub collecting all sorts of useful resources for the board game Spirit Island. You can also find my notes containing thoughts about general concepts and strategies for a variety of Spirits. To know more about this site, or if have any question, please head to the Q&A page, whereas for clarifications about the terminology I use in my guides you can consult the glossary.

Hope you'll enjoy your stay :D
